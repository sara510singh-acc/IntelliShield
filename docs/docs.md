# IntelliShield Risk Assessment

## Stage 1: Define the assessment

This initial self-assessment estimates a user's everyday account and device
security risk. It uses eight multiple-choice questions. Each answer is scored
from 0 (lower risk) to 3 (higher risk); the total score ranges from 0 to 24.

### Questions

1. **How often do you enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on important accounts?**
	- All important accounts (0)
	- Most important accounts (1)
	- A few important accounts (2)
	- None, or I am not sure (3)

2. **How often do you reuse passwords across accounts?**
	- Never; every account has a unique password (0)
	- Only on a small number of low-impact accounts (1)
	- Across several accounts (2)
	- On most accounts, including important ones (3)

3. **How do you create and keep track of passwords?**
	- A password manager generates and stores unique passwords (0)
	- A password manager stores most passwords, but some are reused or predictable (1)
	- I rely on memory, notes, or browser saving and some passwords are predictable (2)
	- I use simple passwords that are easy to remember (3)

4. **When you receive an unexpected email or message asking you to click a link or share information, what do you do?**
	- Verify the sender through a separate trusted channel and report suspicious messages (0)
	- Check the sender and link carefully before taking action (1)
	- Sometimes click or reply before checking (2)
	- Usually trust the message or provide the requested information (3)

5. **How promptly do you install security updates for your phone, computer, browser, and apps?**
	- Automatic updates are enabled, or I install updates promptly (0)
	- I install them within a few weeks (1)
	- I often postpone updates for a long time (2)
	- My devices or apps are unsupported or rarely updated (3)

6. **How well are your devices protected if they are lost or left unattended?**
	- Screen lock is enabled on all devices, with encryption where available (0)
	- Screen lock is enabled on most devices (1)
	- Some devices have a weak lock or no lock (2)
	- My devices are generally accessible without a lock (3)

7. **How prepared are you to recover important accounts if you lose access?**
	- Recovery details are current and I securely keep backup codes where offered (0)
	- Recovery details are current for most important accounts (1)
	- Some recovery details are outdated or unavailable (2)
	- I have not set up recovery options (3)

8. **How do you back up important personal files?**
	- Backups run automatically and include a separate or protected copy (0)
	- I use a regular cloud or external-drive backup (1)
	- I make occasional backups, or only keep files on one device (2)
	- I do not back up important files (3)

### Score interpretation

- **0–6: Lower risk** — Keep these habits in place and review them periodically.
- **7–12: Moderate risk** — Improve the areas where you scored above 0.
- **13–18: High risk** — Prioritize MFA, unique passwords, and timely updates.
- **19–24: Very high risk** — Address the highest-scoring items as soon as possible.

The score is an educational estimate, not a security audit or guarantee. The
assessment should show users their answers and recommend improvements for their
highest-scoring areas rather than presenting the total as a precise probability
of being compromised.
